This folder is an example of what Qasid reads.

Copy the dated folder, rename it to a date you want, and put your own pictures in
beside the .txt files.

  <date>/1.jpg  +  <date>/1.txt    ->  the picture, with that text as its caption
  <date>/1.jpg  on its own         ->  the picture, no caption
  <date>/1.txt  on its own         ->  a plain text message

Posts go out in filename order, starting at the time you set in Qasid, with a gap
between each one. To pin one post to its own time, start its filename with the
time: "07-15 morning.jpg".

Anything else in here — notes, spare files, subfolders — is ignored.
